Put your own product photos here, named by the product CODE from the price list:
  17.jpg  049.png  086-rocket-bomb.jpg  086-king-of-king.jpg
Then run:  python tools/add-photos.py   (from the website folder)
It resizes them to 800x550 WebP and links them in data/products.js,
replacing the Unsplash photo for those products.
